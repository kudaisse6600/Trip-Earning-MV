How this works and what it can't do

Phones have no link to the engine computer, so a true RPM reading is impossible from a browser. This estimate finds the strongest repeating low-frequency sound in the cabin and converts it with RPM = firings/s × 120 ÷ cylinders. It only shows a number when the signal is clearly periodic; otherwise it shows a dash rather than guessing.

Hybrid vehicles: when the petrol engine is off, there is no firing sound and no RPM exists to measure. The electric motor's whine is not engine RPM, and the estimator will usually show nothing or an unrelated value. Treat it as unavailable in EV mode.

Road noise, music, air-con and the phone's position all affect it. Expect it to be rough at best, and never use it for anything that matters.
